Privacy Policy

This draft concerns the informational GB GAMER BROS™ game-preview site at gb.happydude.ca. It does not describe the separate HAPPY DUDE® shop, its checkout, payments or customer accounts.

Scope of the review

The current static preview code does not expose a visitor account, game registration or checkout form. It uses temporary browser session storage for a website revision/reload check.
